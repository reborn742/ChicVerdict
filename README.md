# ChicVerdict — Your Personal AI Stylist

**Your wardrobe. Your occasion. An honest verdict.**

ChicVerdict is an AI-powered personal styling app that analyzes clothing items from your wardrobe and recommends coordinated outfits based on the occasion and your age. Instead of generic fashion compliments, it aims to deliver direct feedback, explain styling choices, and help you make better outfit decisions using clothes you already own.

**Live Demo:** https://chic-verdict.vercel.app

## What It Does

- **AI Outfit Recommendations** — Analyze uploaded clothing items and suggest a coordinated look for a specific occasion.
- **Age-Aware Styling** — Evaluate whether an outfit suits the user's stated age, alongside the occasion.
- **Honest Style Verdicts** — Receive a verdict, a stylist's note, a detailed explanation, and an honesty score.
- **Wardrobe Organization** — Work with clothing categories such as tops, bottoms, dresses, footwear, bags, and accessories.
- **Image Enhancement** — Use AI-powered image enhancement to improve the presentation of wardrobe photos.
- **Image Editing and Camera Input** — Supporting components provide image editing and camera-based input workflows.
- **Outfit Archive** — The app's data model supports saving outfit recommendations with their occasion and associated images.

## How It Works

1. **Add wardrobe items** by supplying images of available clothing and accessories.
2. **Choose the occasion** and enter your age.
3. **Analyze your wardrobe** using multimodal AI to evaluate the clothing items together.
4. **Review the recommendation**, including the selected pieces, overall verdict, age-appropriateness assessment, and styling rationale.
5. **Refine your wardrobe workflow** using the app's image tools and outfit organization features.
